For TopFlight Builders, always lead a report, summary or artifact with **non-branded clicks and
channel engagement quality**. Never lead with total impressions or average position.

**Why:** both of the forbidden metrics moved sharply in Q3 2026 for reasons unrelated to whether
the business gets work. The city-by-service page rollout indexed on Jul 4 and added thousands of
impressions ranking 10th to 25th, which lifted total impressions ~45x and dragged average position
from 5.2 to 15.0. Neither number changed how many customers called. Leading with them tells a story
that is arithmetic, not performance, and it flatters a quarter that produced six non-branded clicks.

**How to apply:** headline tiles and opening paragraph carry non-branded clicks, branded vs
non-branded split, and per-channel engagement rate and average engagement time. Impressions and
average position may appear later as diagnostics, explicitly labelled as such. This was applied to
the punch-list artifact on 2026-09-03; the masthead and tiles were rebuilt for it.
